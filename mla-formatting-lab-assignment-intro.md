# MLA Formatting Lab

**Due:** [due date]
**Worth:** [points / participation credit]

## Why this matters

Every essay you turn in for this course needs to follow MLA format. Instead of just reading a list of rules, this lab hands you a document with the formatting mistakes already in it and walks you through fixing each one yourself — margins, font, spacing, the heading, the title, and the paragraph indent. By the end, you will have practiced the exact formatting your essays need all semester.

## What you'll do

- Open the MLA Formatting Lab and enter your name to begin. You don't need Google Docs — the lab includes its own practice workspace.
- Work through nine guided steps in the MLA Coach panel: margins, font, font size, line spacing, the heading date, the running header, title capitalization, title alignment, and the paragraph indent.
- Use the **I'm Stuck** button anytime you need a hint. Using it will not cost you credit.
- Once all nine steps are complete, you'll unlock a completion certificate.

## How to submit

1. On the final screen, click **View Completion Certificate**.
2. Click **Save Certificate as PDF** and save the file.
3. Upload that PDF to this assignment in Blackboard. Your certificate is your proof of completion — nothing else needs to be turned in.

## One more thing

After you submit your certificate, open the MLA Essay Template linked on the certificate screen. It opens your own copy automatically, already filled in with your name and today's date — save it somewhere you'll be able to find all semester, since you'll use this same template for every MLA essay in this course.

Note: this lab covers MLA *page formatting* only. In-text citations and the Works Cited page are not part of this activity.
